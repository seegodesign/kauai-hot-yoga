## Retreat overview

Our journey begins in the Andean city of Cusco, the historic capital of the Inca Empire. From Cusco, we travel into the Sacred Valley to Hotel La Casona de Yucay, surrounded by the splendor of the Andes.

During our stay, guests can enjoy nourishing meals at Restaurante Tikka and relaxing hotel amenities, including a swimming pool, jacuzzi, and therapeutic massages. From this peaceful home base, we’ll set out on intentionally curated excursions throughout the Sacred Valley.

## A week of movement and discovery

The journey is designed to balance adventure with restoration. Days will weave together yoga and movement, visits to extraordinary cultural and natural sites, locally rooted experiences, delicious meals, and spacious time to rest.

Planned experiences include Machu Picchu, Rainbow Mountain, the salt mines of Maras, the Urubamba Farmers Market, Seminario Ceramics Center, and a presentation on traditional weaving with the women of Chinchero. The final schedule will be shared with registered guests as the retreat approaches.

## Your home in the Sacred Valley

Hotel La Casona de Yucay sits in the heart of the Sacred Valley, offering a quiet place to land between adventures. Its gardens and mountain setting create a restorative backdrop for daily practice, shared meals, and connection with the group.
